Timeline

Explicitly dated milestones drawn from sourced entries across the archive, oldest first. Only dates stated in an entry's own sources appear here — nothing is estimated or invented.

  • 1959Term 'hack' documented in MIT student groups' vocabulary, including TMRC. — The Hacker Ethic: MIT's Tech Model Railroad Club
  • 1963-08-06Born in Los Angeles, California. — Kevin Mitnick: A Complete History
  • 1964-10-23Born in Nagoya, Japan. — Tsutomu Shimomura
  • 1965-11-08Born in the United States, son of Bell Labs/NSA computer scientist Robert Morris. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 1970-12-01Born in Alabama. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 1977Alexander Peslyak is born. — Solar Designer
  • 1979-02-07Born in San Francisco, California. — Dan Kaminsky
  • 1981CCC founded in Berlin. — The Chaos Computer Club (CCC)
  • 1983Announces the GNU Project. — Richard Stallman, the GNU Project, and Free Software
  • 1984The 'BTX-Hack' publicly demonstrates a flaw in Germany's Bildschirmtext system. — The Chaos Computer Club (CCC)
  • 1984cDc founded in Lubbock, Texas. — The Cult of the Dead Cow (cDc) and Hacktivism
  • 1984Legion of Doom founded by Lex Luthor. — Legion of Doom (LOD): The Masters of the Digital Universe
  • 1984Steven Levy publishes 'Hackers: Heroes of the Computer Revolution', codifying the Hacker Ethic. — The Hacker Ethic: MIT's Tech Model Railroad Club
  • 19842600: The Hacker Quarterly founded by Eric Corley ('Emmanuel Goldstein'). — 2600: The Hacker Quarterly
  • 1985-11-17First issue of Phrack released. — Phrack Magazine: The Hacker's Ezine
  • 1986Begins investigating a 75-cent accounting discrepancy at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. — Clifford Stoll and 'The Cuckoo's Egg'
  • 1986Published in Phrack under the handle 'The Mentor'. — The Hacker Manifesto: 'The Conscience of a Hacker'
  • 1988Convicted for the DEC intrusion: 12 months prison, 3 years supervised release. — Kevin Mitnick: A Complete History
  • 1988As a Cornell graduate student, releases a self-replicating program (the Morris Worm) from MIT; a bug causes it to spread uncontrollably. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 1988-11-02Morris Worm released, infects an estimated 10% of the ~60,000 hosts then on the internet. — The Morris Worm: The Internet's First Wake-Up Call
  • 1989Publishes 'The Cuckoo's Egg'. — Clifford Stoll and 'The Cuckoo's Egg'
  • 1989Joined UC San Diego's physics department and became a senior fellow at the San Diego Supercomputer Center. — Tsutomu Shimomura
  • 1990-12Sentenced to three years probation, 400 hours community service, and a $10,050 fine as the first person convicted under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 1992Key MOD members arrested in a federal crackdown. — Masters of Deception: The New York Hacker Wars
  • 1992Testified before the U.S. Congress on cellular telephone security and privacy vulnerabilities. — Tsutomu Shimomura
  • 1993First DEF CON held in Las Vegas, organized by Jeff Moss ('The Dark Tangent'). — DEF CON: The World's Most Famous Hacker Con
  • 1994Adopts the handle "Solar Designer" to register on private BBSes. — Solar Designer
  • 1994-12-25An intruder (later identified as Kevin Mitnick) launched an IP-spoofing attack against Shimomura's home computer systems. — Tsutomu Shimomura
  • 1995Publishes "How to Write Buffer Overflows," an early technical paper on buffer-overflow exploitation. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 1995Co-founds Viaweb with Paul Graham. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 1995-01-23CERT/CC issued Advisory CA-1995-01 on IP spoofing, around the same time as New York Times coverage of the break-in. — Tsutomu Shimomura
  • 1995-02-15Arrested by the FBI in Raleigh, North Carolina. — Kevin Mitnick: A Complete History
  • 1995-02-15FBI agents arrested Kevin Mitnick in Raleigh, North Carolina, with Shimomura having assisted in tracking him. — Tsutomu Shimomura
  • 1996cDc coins the term 'hacktivism'. — The Cult of the Dead Cow (cDc) and Hacktivism
  • 1996First released by Solar Designer (Alexander Peslyak). — John the Ripper: The Timeless Password Cracker
  • 1996Releases John the Ripper, a password-cracking tool for Unix systems. — Solar Designer
  • 1996-01Shimomura and John Markoff published 'Takedown.' — Tsutomu Shimomura
  • 1997Nmap source code first published in Phrack Magazine, issue 51. — Nmap: The Network Mapper
  • 1997Publishes early return-into-libc buffer overflow exploits and a Linux kernel non-executable-memory hardening patch. — Solar Designer
  • 1998Back Orifice released at DEF CON 6. — The Cult of the Dead Cow (cDc) and Hacktivism
  • 1998First release, under the name Ethereal. — Wireshark: Sniffing the Wires
  • 1998Yahoo! acquires Viaweb for about $49 million; relaunched as Yahoo! Store. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 1998-05-19Seven L0pht members testify before the U.S. Senate Committee on Governmental Affairs. — L0pht Heavy Industries: Advising the Senate
  • 1998-05-19Testifies with six other L0pht members before the U.S. Senate Committee on Governmental Affairs. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 1999Earns a PhD from Harvard University. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 1999Founds and leads the Openwall Project. — Solar Designer
  • 2000Released; re-enters the security industry as a consultant. — Kevin Mitnick: A Complete History
  • 2002Hacks The New York Times intranet. — Adrian Lamo: The 'Homeless Hacker'
  • 2002Joined penetration-testing firm @stake as a security researcher. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2003H. D. Moore starts the Metasploit Project. — The Metasploit Framework: Democratizing Penetration Testing
  • 2003Founded the Metasploit Project in the summer of 2003 as a public resource for exploit code and vulnerability research. — H. D. Moore
  • 2003Founds Openwall, Inc. and becomes its CTO. — Solar Designer
  • 2004Metasploit Framework 2.0 released, rewritten in Ruby. — The Metasploit Framework: Democratizing Penetration Testing
  • 2004-10Moved to Symantec after its acquisition of @stake and founded Symantec's Vulnerability Research program. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2005Used DNS cache snooping to estimate the number of computers infected by the Sony BMG rootkit at several hundred thousand. — Dan Kaminsky
  • 2005Hired as Director of Security Research at BreakingPoint Systems in Austin, Texas. — H. D. Moore
  • 2005Co-founds the startup accelerator Y Combinator. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 2006WikiLeaks founded by Julian Assange. — WikiLeaks & Julian Assange: Radical Transparency
  • 2006Renamed Wireshark after a trademark dispute. — Wireshark: Sniffing the Wires
  • 2006Receives tenure as a professor in MIT's Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 2006-07Ran the "Month of Browser Bugs," publishing a new browser vulnerability every day for the month of July. — H. D. Moore
  • 2006-08-03Presented the "Blue Pill" hardware-virtualization rootkit concept and a Windows Vista kernel-protection bypass at Black Hat Briefings in Las Vegas. — Joanna Rutkowska
  • 2007First to carrier-unlock the original iPhone. — George 'geohot' Hotz: The iPhone Jailbreaker
  • 2007Joined Microsoft as a security strategist, later founding the Microsoft Vulnerability Research (MSVR) program. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2007-04Founded Invisible Things Lab (ITL) in Warsaw, Poland, focused on OS and VMM security research. — Joanna Rutkowska
  • 2008Project Chanology launched against the Church of Scientology. — Anonymous: From 4chan Pranksters to Global Hacktivists
  • 2008Co-founds the oss-security mailing list. — Solar Designer
  • 2008-07-08Coordinated, multi-vendor patches for the DNS cache-poisoning flaw he discovered (CVE-2008-1447 / CERT VU#800113) were released simultaneously across the industry. — Dan Kaminsky
  • 2008-08Publicly detailed the DNS vulnerability in the "Black Ops 2008" talk at Black Hat USA, and later at DEF CON 16 and Black Hat Japan. — Dan Kaminsky
  • 2009Acquired by Rapid7. — The Metasploit Framework: Democratizing Penetration Testing
  • 2009Helped identify a network-detectable signature for Conficker-infected hosts, later integrated into Nmap and Nessus. — Dan Kaminsky
  • 2009Coined the term "evil maid attack" in a blog post describing firmware/USB-based compromise of encrypted, unattended machines. — Joanna Rutkowska
  • 2009Receives the Pwnie Award Lifetime Achievement Award at Black Hat. — Solar Designer
  • 2009-10Rapid7 acquired the Metasploit Project; Moore joined Rapid7 and remained chief architect of the Metasploit Framework. — H. D. Moore
  • 2010Reports Chelsea Manning's disclosures to the FBI. — Adrian Lamo: The 'Homeless Hacker'
  • 2010Bypasses PlayStation 3 security; sued by Sony. — George 'geohot' Hotz: The iPhone Jailbreaker
  • 2010Publishes the Afghan War Diary and Iraq War Logs, leaked by Chelsea Manning. — WikiLeaks & Julian Assange: Radical Transparency
  • 2010Stuxnet discovered publicly; attributed to a joint U.S.-Israeli operation targeting Iran's Natanz facility. — Stuxnet: The World's First Digital Weapon
  • 2010Began developing Qubes OS with Rafal Wojtczuk, a Xen-based desktop OS using compartmentalized virtual machines for security. — Joanna Rutkowska
  • 2010Joins DARPA as a cybersecurity program manager. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 2011Launches DARPA's Cyber Fast Track program. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 2011-08-03Led the launch of the Microsoft BlueHat Prize, offering a $200,000 grand prize for defensive security research. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2012-09-03Qubes OS reached its 1.0 release. — Joanna Rutkowska
  • 2013Rapid7's Project Sonar, an internet-wide scanning research effort led by Moore, launched as a successor to the earlier Critical.IO scanning project. — H. D. Moore
  • 2013Created and launched Microsoft's first bug bounty program. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2014-05Left Microsoft to become Chief Policy Officer at HackerOne. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2015-10Published the paper "Intel x86 considered harmful," analyzing systemic security weaknesses in the x86 platform. — Joanna Rutkowska
  • 2016Stepped down as CEO and CTO of Neofocal Systems. — Tsutomu Shimomura
  • 2016-01-29Departed Rapid7 after six years to join a venture capital firm focused on early-stage security startups. — H. D. Moore
  • 2016-04Founded Luta Security, a vulnerability disclosure and bug bounty consultancy. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2016-04-18'Hack the Pentagon' pilot program launched, running through May 12, 2016; 138 of 250 submitted reports confirmed as legitimate vulnerabilities. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2017-05Discovers the WannaCry kill switch domain. — Marcus Hutchins: The Accidental Hero of WannaCry
  • 2017-05-08Hired by Stripe as head of security. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 2017-12-06Wassenaar Arrangement delegates agreed on revised 'intrusion software' export-control language after years of advocacy involving Moussouris. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2018Dies at age 37. — Adrian Lamo: The 'Homeless Hacker'
  • 2018Founded Rumble, Inc., an IT asset-discovery and network-visibility company. — H. D. Moore
  • 2018-03-09Openwall releases yescrypt 1.0.0, the password-hashing scheme he designed. — Solar Designer
  • 2018-10-25Announced she was stepping down as lead of Qubes OS and joining the Golem Project as Chief Strategy Officer and Chief Security Officer. — Joanna Rutkowska
  • 2019Elected to the National Academy of Engineering. — Robert Tappan Morris
  • 2020-11-17Hired by Twitter as head of security, reporting to CEO Jack Dorsey. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 2021Posthumously inducted into the Internet Hall of Fame; Human Security established the Dan Kaminsky Fellowship in his honor. — Dan Kaminsky
  • 2021-04-23Died in San Francisco at age 42; cause reported as diabetic ketoacidosis. — Dan Kaminsky
  • 2021-05-25Testified before the U.S. House Committee on Science, Space, and Technology on vulnerability disclosure policy. — Katie Moussouris
  • 2022Rumble, Inc. renamed runZero, Inc., with Moore continuing as founder and CEO. — H. D. Moore
  • 2022-01Fired from Twitter; the company cites poor performance. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 2022-07Files a whistleblower disclosure with the SEC, DOJ, FTC and congressional committees regarding Twitter. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 2022-08-23Whistleblower complaint becomes public after being reported by The Washington Post and CNN. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 2022-09-13Testifies before the U.S. Senate Judiciary Committee about Twitter's security practices. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
  • 2023Joins CIQ's Rocky Linux Security effort. — Solar Designer
  • 2023-07-16Dies at 59 from pancreatic cancer. — Kevin Mitnick: A Complete History
  • 2024-08-08Appointed Chief Information Officer of DARPA. — Peiter "Mudge" Zatko
