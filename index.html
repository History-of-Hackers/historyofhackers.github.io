Recently added

Marion Marschalek

Person · 2011-Present

Marion Marschalek is an Austrian-born reverse engineer and malware analyst whose confirmed career spans Ikarus Security Software, Intel's STORM hardware-security team, and AWS GuardDuty, and who now runs her own reverse-engineering consultancy, Hack & Cheese, while remaining active in the BlackHoodie women-in-reverse-engineering community.

Peter Van Eeckhoutte

Person · 2007-Present

Peter Van Eeckhoutte is a Belgian security researcher and trainer who founded the Corelan Security Team in 2009 and authored the widely used Corelan Exploit Writing Tutorials, becoming a leading figure in Windows exploit-development education.

Rain Forest Puppy

Person · 1998-2002

Rain Forest Puppy (rfp) was the pseudonym of a security researcher who published one of the earliest widely-read technical descriptions of SQL injection, built the Whisker web-vulnerability scanner, and proposed RFPolicy, an early formal framework for coordinating vulnerability disclosure with vendors.

Renaud Deraison

Person · 1998-Present

Renaud Deraison is a French-born security researcher who created the Nessus vulnerability scanner as a lycée student in 1998 and went on to co-found Tenable Network Security in 2002, serving as the company's chief technology officer through its 2018 Nasdaq IPO before moving into advisory and board roles across the cybersecurity industry.

Roberto Preatoni

Person · 2002-Present

Italian penetration tester and internet entrepreneur best known as the founder of Zone-H, the long-running website-defacement archive, and of WabiSabiLabi, an early online vulnerability marketplace; in November 2007 he was arrested in Milan as part of the wide-ranging Telecom Italia "Tiger Team" wiretapping and corporate-espionage investigation.

Silvio Cesare

Person · 1993-Present

Silvio Cesare is an Australian security researcher active since the early 1990s, known for Phrack articles on binary infection techniques, a Snort IDS-evasion discovery, and later academic work on malware classification at Deakin University; he now runs the Canberra security firm InfoSect.
