Joanna Rutkowska

Person · 2000s-Present

Joanna Rutkowska is a Polish computer security researcher best known for the 2006 "Blue Pill" hardware-virtualization rootkit concept, for founding Invisible Things Lab, and for creating the security-by-isolation desktop operating system Qubes OS.

Katie Moussouris

Person · 2000s-Present

Katie Moussouris is an American security researcher and policy advocate who created Microsoft's first bug bounty program, helped design the U.S. Department of Defense's "Hack the Pentagon" pilot, and founded the vulnerability-disclosure consultancy Luta Security.

Peiter "Mudge" Zatko

Person · 1990s-Present

Peiter "Mudge" Zatko is an American security researcher who rose to prominence as a leading member of the L0pht hacker collective in the 1990s, later ran cybersecurity programs at DARPA and held security leadership roles at Google, Stripe and Twitter, and in 2022 became internationally known as the whistleblower who accused Twitter of severe security and privacy failures before Congress, the SEC and the FTC.

Robert Tappan Morris

Person · 1980s-Present

Robert Tappan Morris is an American computer scientist who, as a Cornell graduate student in 1988, released the first worm to spread widely across the early Internet and became the first person convicted under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act; he later co-founded Viaweb and Y Combinator and is now a tenured MIT professor of computer science.

Solar Designer

Person · 1990s-Present

Solar Designer (Alexander Peslyak) created the John the Ripper password cracker in 1996 and founded the Openwall Project, becoming one of the most influential figures in open-source Linux security hardening and exploitation research.

Tsutomu Shimomura

Person · 1980s-Present

A computational physicist and security researcher at the San Diego Supercomputer Center, Shimomura became internationally known after a December 1994 attack on his own home systems led him to help the FBI track and arrest fugitive hacker Kevin Mitnick, a chase he later recounted in the book Takedown.
