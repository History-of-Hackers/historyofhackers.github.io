Kevin Mahaffey

Person · 2009-Present

Kevin Mahaffey is a cybersecurity entrepreneur and researcher best known as co-founder and CTO of Lookout, the mobile security company, and for co-discovering critical remote-exploit vulnerabilities in the Tesla Model S alongside researcher Marc Rogers in 2015.

Nicolas Waisman

Person · 2004-Present

Nicolas "Nico" Waisman is an Argentine security researcher who spent over a decade at Immunity, Inc. — rising to Vice President of Latin America — where he built a reputation as a globally recognized heap-exploitation expert, later contributing to GitHub's Security Lab research team.
