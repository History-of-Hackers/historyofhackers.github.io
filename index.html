Recently added

Keen Security Lab

Group · 2016-Present

Tencent Keen Security Lab is a China-based vulnerability research team best known for a series of published remote and physical attacks against Tesla, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, and Lexus vehicles, disclosed responsibly to manufacturers between 2016 and 2021.

LulzSec

Group · 2011

LulzSec (Lulz Security) was a short-lived hacktivist collective active from May to June 2011, loosely linked to Anonymous, that carried out a rapid string of high-profile breaches and DDoS attacks against Sony, PBS, Fox, InfraGard, Arizona DPS, and others before publicly disbanding after 50 days.

Phone Losers of America

Group · 1994-Present

Phone Losers of America (PLA) is a long-running phone phreaking and prank-call community founded in 1994 by Brad Carter (also known online as RBCP) and a collaborator known as Zak (el_jefe), which grew from a photocopied hacking zine into an internet radio show and podcast still active today.

Project Zero

Group · 2014-Present

Project Zero is a team of security researchers employed by Google, founded in 2014 to find and privately report zero-day vulnerabilities in any software, not just Google's own, under a public 90-day disclosure deadline.

ShadowCrew

Group · 2002-2004

ShadowCrew was an online carding and identity-fraud forum active from 2002 to 2004 whose members traded stolen credit card data, forged documents, and hacking tutorials; it was dismantled in October 2004 by the U.S. Secret Service's Operation Firewall, which led to 19 federal indictments and later revealed that a top member, Albert Gonzalez, had been a cooperating government informant.

Uninformed

Publication · 2005-2008

Uninformed (uninformed.org) was a technical journal published from 2005 to 2008 covering exploitation techniques, reverse engineering, and low-level Windows security research, producing 10 volumes and 47 articles before going dormant.
