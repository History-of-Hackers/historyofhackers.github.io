Jennifer Granick

Person · 1990s-Present

Jennifer Granick is an American attorney who began her career defending accused computer hackers in the 1990s and went on to become one of the United States' leading legal voices on digital civil liberties, directing Stanford Law School's Center for Internet and Society and holding senior counsel roles at the Electronic Frontier Foundation and the ACLU.

Runa Sandvik

Person · 2000s-Present

Runa Sandvik is a Norwegian-American computer security researcher who moved from Tor Project development and smart-weapon hacking research into building digital-security programs for newsrooms, most notably as director of information security at The New York Times.
