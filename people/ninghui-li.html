Ninghui Li

Li’s research and service have helped shape access-control and privacy research as well as the security research community.

Ninghui Li is a computer-security researcher and professor associated with Purdue University. His research includes access control, trust management, distributed-system security, applied cryptography, and privacy.

Policy and trust

Li’s work studies how systems express, delegate, and enforce security policy. Alongside his research, he has served the field through ACM SIGSAC and editorial leadership for ACM Transactions on Privacy and Security.
