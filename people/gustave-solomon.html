Gustave Solomon

Error correction is a fundamental part of trustworthy digital storage and communication. Solomon’s work made an enduring contribution to the reliability layer on which computing systems depend.

Gustave Solomon was a american mathematician and electrical engineer who co-invented Reed–Solomon error-correcting codes with Irving S. Reed.

Historical contribution

Error correction is a fundamental part of trustworthy digital storage and communication. Solomon’s work made an enduring contribution to the reliability layer on which computing systems depend.

In memoriam

Gustave Solomon died in 1996. This archive remembers their contribution with care and credits the historical record on which this account relies.
