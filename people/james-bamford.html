James Bamford

Bamford’s reporting provides important context for the institutional history behind surveillance, cryptography, and the security state that surrounds modern computer security.

James Bamford is an investigative journalist and author known for book-length reporting on the National Security Agency and United States signals intelligence. He has also worked as an investigative producer and written for major magazines.

Security institutions in public view

Bamford’s books make intelligence institutions and their technical capabilities legible to a general audience. That history is relevant to hacker culture because security research, cryptography, surveillance, and state power repeatedly intersect.

Timeline

  • 1982Publishes The Puzzle Palace, a book on the National Security Agency.
  • 2002Publishes Body of Secrets, expanding his reporting on the NSA and intelligence history.
