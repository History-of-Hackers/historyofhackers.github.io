Stefan Savage

Savage helped establish data-driven, ecosystem-level analysis as a core method for understanding online abuse and defensive priorities.

Stefan Savage is a computer-security researcher at the University of California, San Diego. His work studies how Internet-scale systems fail and how abuse operates across technical and economic infrastructure.

Measuring abuse

Rather than treating malicious activity as isolated incidents, Savage’s research has used measurement to map relationships among malware, hosting, advertising, and payment systems. This perspective has helped security teams focus on intervention points beyond a single compromised machine.

Systems perspective

His work illustrates the value of reproducible measurement and interdisciplinary thinking in security research.
