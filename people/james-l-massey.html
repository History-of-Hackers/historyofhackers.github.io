James L. Massey

Massey helped establish the intellectual link between information theory, coding theory and cryptography. His teaching and research influenced generations of engineers who build reliable and secure communications systems.

Information theory and cryptology

James L. Massey was an information theorist and cryptographer who held academic positions in the United States and Switzerland, including at ETH Zurich. His work addressed coding theory, information theory and cryptology, disciplines that share the goal of reasoning precisely about communication under noise, uncertainty and adversarial interference.

Academic legacy

Massey is remembered for influential scholarship and teaching in cryptology and coding theory. The ETH Zurich obituary notes his long connection to the institution; his professional legacy is reflected in the continuing use of information-theoretic methods in secure communications and cryptographic analysis.
