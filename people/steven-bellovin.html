Steven M. Bellovin

Bellovin’s work has documented and analyzed security flaws in networked systems across decades of Internet growth, helping define the discipline of network security.

Steven M. Bellovin is a computer-security researcher and professor at Columbia University. His work on network security, Internet protocols, and privacy has helped the field understand how design assumptions turn into operational vulnerabilities.

Internet security

Bellovin’s 1989 paper on security problems in the TCP/IP protocol suite remains a historical reference for the need to analyze protocol ecosystems, not just individual implementations.

Public scholarship

His research and writing have connected technical detail with the policy and operational questions raised by widely deployed networks.
