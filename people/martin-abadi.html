Martín Abadi

Abadi co-developed widely used formal approaches to authentication-protocol analysis, helping make security claims about protocols explicit and open to scrutiny.

Martín Abadi is a computer scientist whose research has crossed formal methods, programming languages, cryptography, and security. His work on protocol logics became a central reference point for analyzing authentication designs.

Reasoning about protocols

With Michael Burrows and Roger Needham, Abadi developed BAN logic, a notation and reasoning system for authentication protocols. Its importance lies in making assumptions, beliefs, and desired guarantees visible for analysis.

Formal security research

Abadi’s later work has continued to connect language-based methods and formal models with practical questions of system and protocol security.
