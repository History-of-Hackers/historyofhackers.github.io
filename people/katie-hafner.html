Katie Hafner

Hafner's reporting helped establish a documentary record of early network-intrusion cases and hacker culture for a general readership.

Katie Hafner is a journalist and author. With John Markoff, she wrote Cyberpunk: Outlaws and Hackers on the Computer Frontier, first published in 1991.

Documenting an emerging culture

The book followed several computer-intrusion cases and placed them in the technological and social context of the 1980s. It remains an important contemporary source for the period's public understanding of hackers and network security.
