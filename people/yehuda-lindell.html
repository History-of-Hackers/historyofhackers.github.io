Yehuda Lindell

Yehuda Lindell is included for documented contributions to the technical and formal foundations of computer security and cryptography.

Yehuda Lindell is a cryptographer and Bar-Ilan University professor whose research focuses on secure multiparty computation and practical cryptographic protocols. He has also contributed to academic education through widely used cryptography texts.

Security relevance

Lindell’s work connects provable-security techniques with implementations that protect keys and distributed computations. It illustrates how formal cryptographic research can inform deployed systems.

Timeline

  • 2002Receives a Ph.D. from the Weizmann Institute.
  • 2007Co-authors Introduction to Modern Cryptography with Jonathan Katz.
  • 2015Co-founds Unbound Security.
  • 2022Becomes Head of Cryptography at Coinbase.
