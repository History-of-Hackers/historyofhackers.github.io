David Naccache

Naccache's work joins cryptographic construction with the realities of constrained devices and physical attack surfaces. That bridge matters wherever cryptography is implemented in smart cards, mobile devices and embedded products.

Research and practice

David Naccache heads the Information Security Group at ENS Paris. The group's profile describes research interests including code security, forensics and vulnerability detection, alongside a career in industry at Gemplus, Philips and Thomson.

Cryptography for embedded systems

Naccache has published on practical public-key constructions, signatures and the security of embedded systems. ENS's CASCADE research material identifies work on cryptography for embedded-system security, including privacy and integrity of code and confidence in cryptographic implementations.
