Abraham Lempel

Lossless compression is core infrastructure for computing and communications. Lempel’s work, developed with Jacob Ziv, became a durable foundation for widely deployed digital formats and transmission systems.

Abraham Lempel was a israeli computer scientist and information theorist who co-created the Lempel–Ziv family of lossless compression algorithms.

Historical contribution

Lossless compression is core infrastructure for computing and communications. Lempel’s work, developed with Jacob Ziv, became a durable foundation for widely deployed digital formats and transmission systems.

In memoriam

Abraham Lempel died in 2023. This archive remembers their contribution with care and credits the historical record on which this account relies.
