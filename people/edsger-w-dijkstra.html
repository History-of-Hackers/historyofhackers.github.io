Edsger W. Dijkstra

Dijkstra treated programming as an intellectual discipline in which clarity and proof matter. That insistence remains directly relevant to building software whose security properties can be understood and maintained.

Edsger W. Dijkstra made foundational contributions to algorithms and programming methodology, including the shortest-path algorithm that bears his name. He also argued forcefully for structured programming and formal reasoning about programs.

Reliable systems

His essays and lectures connected program construction to correctness. Security engineering inherits this concern: a system cannot reliably enforce a property that its builders cannot state or reason about.

In memoriam

Dijkstra died in 2002. His papers and lectures continue to influence computer-science education.
