Eric Rescorla

Rescorla's work has influenced the protocols that protect everyday web traffic; his standards and security analysis connect cryptographic design with deployable Internet engineering.

Internet security engineering

Eric Rescorla is an Internet-security researcher and protocol engineer. He has worked on the design, analysis, and deployment of security mechanisms for the web and Internet communications.

TLS

Rescorla was a co-author of TLS 1.3, the IETF standard that modernized the protocol used to protect a substantial share of web traffic. The work aimed to simplify negotiation, remove obsolete cryptographic options, and strengthen privacy properties.

Standards and practice

His writing and IETF contributions illustrate a central feature of applied cryptography: a secure design must also be implementable, interoperable, and maintainable across a diverse global network.
