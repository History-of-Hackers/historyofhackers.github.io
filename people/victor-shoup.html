Victor Shoup

Victor Shoup is included for documented contributions to the technical and formal foundations of computer security and cryptography.

Victor Shoup is a professor at NYU Courant whose research covers cryptography, computational number theory, and algorithms. He also created NTL, a widely used open-source C++ library for number-theory computations.

Security relevance

Public-key cryptography depends heavily on reliable arithmetic over large integers and finite fields. Shoup’s research and software made these mathematical foundations more accessible to researchers and implementers.

Timeline

  • 1989Receives a Ph.D. in computer science from the University of Wisconsin–Madison.
  • 1990sDevelops NTL, a number-theory library used in cryptographic research and implementation.
  • 2001Publishes a graduate course text on applied cryptography.
  • 2004Serves as editor for ISO 18033-2 on public-key encryption.
