Peter G. Neumann

Neumann's work joins secure-system design with reliability, safety, and societal risk. It has helped keep attention on the ways technical failures and security failures can interact.

Peter G. Neumann is a computer scientist associated with SRI International's Computer Science Laboratory. His research interests include security, survivability, reliability, safety, formal methods, and software engineering.

Secure systems and risk

Neumann worked on secure-system architectures and, at SRI, contributed to research on intrusion detection and survivable systems. NIST recognized his long-term contributions to computer-security solutions with the 2002 Computer System Security Award.

Public discussion of failures

He has also moderated the ACM RISKS Forum and authored Computer-Related Risks, helping sustain a public technical record of computer-system failures, hazards, and security concerns.
