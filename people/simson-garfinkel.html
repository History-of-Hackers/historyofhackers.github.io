Simson L. Garfinkel

Garfinkel has connected technical research, writing, and policy-oriented analysis, making complex questions of privacy and digital evidence accessible to broader audiences.

Simson L. Garfinkel is a computer scientist, journalist, and author whose work crosses digital forensics, privacy, and computer security. He has written for technical and general audiences while contributing to research on digital evidence and data protection.

Explaining security

Security history depends on people who can investigate systems and explain why the findings matter. Garfinkel’s work has connected forensic practice and privacy research with public-facing writing.

Interdisciplinary record

His career shows how computer-security research can inform journalism, public policy, and the design of tools for handling digital evidence.
