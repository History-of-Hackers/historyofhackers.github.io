Tal Rabin

Rabin's work helps make cryptographic systems resilient when trust is distributed: a core concern for key management, secure services, and multi-party protocols.

Tal Rabin is a computer scientist whose research focuses on cryptography, distributed systems, and secure computation. Her work examines how security properties can survive when computation and key material are shared among multiple parties.

Distributed trust

Threshold and multiparty techniques reduce dependence on a single key holder or service. Rabin's research has contributed to the theory and practice of building such systems with clearly defined adversaries and guarantees.

Security history

Her work belongs in the history of security engineering because modern infrastructure increasingly relies on distributed custody, fault tolerance, and collaboration without universal trust.
