Lance Spitzner

The Honeynet Project helped establish deliberately instrumented systems as a shared research method for observing attacks. Spitzner also became an influential educator on security awareness and human risk.

Lance Spitzner is a security practitioner and educator who founded the Honeynet Project. His early work used high-interaction honeynets to study attacker activity and improve defensive understanding.

Honeynets as a research method

A honeynet is a controlled network designed to be observed when it is probed or compromised. The project brought together volunteers, research, and practical operational lessons around collecting and interpreting this evidence responsibly.

Teaching and human risk

Spitzner later focused on security culture, awareness, and human risk at SANS. Technical defenses and the decisions people make around them are both part of an effective security program.
