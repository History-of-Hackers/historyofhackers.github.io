Dieter Gollmann

Gollmann’s research and widely used security-engineering texts connect foundational concepts to deployed systems.

Dieter Gollmann is a computer-security scholar associated with Hamburg University of Technology and SBA Research. His work has covered communications security, software security, application security, and security in distributed systems.

Security engineering across layers

Gollmann’s writing emphasizes that sound security depends on the interaction of mechanisms, systems, and operational context. His textbook Computer Security has been used to introduce the discipline’s concepts and engineering trade-offs.
