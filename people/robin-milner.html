Robin Milner

Milner supplied formalisms for reasoning about interacting processes, an essential challenge for protocols and distributed systems. Those ideas inform the formal analysis used in modern security research.

Robin Milner was a British computer scientist whose work on logic, programming languages, and concurrency had lasting influence on formal methods. His contributions include LCF theorem proving, the ML language, and the Calculus of Communicating Systems.

Processes and protocols

Security protocols describe independent parties that exchange messages under defined rules. Milner's process-calculus work offered a rigorous way to describe and reason about such communicating processes.

In memoriam

Milner died in 2010. His work remains foundational in programming-language theory, formal verification, and analysis of concurrent systems.
