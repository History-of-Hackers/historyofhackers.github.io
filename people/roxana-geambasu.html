Roxana Geambasu

Geambasu connects systems construction with privacy outcomes, emphasizing tools and abstractions that let people and institutions understand and constrain data use.

Roxana Geambasu is an associate professor of computer science at Columbia University. Her research spans systems, security, privacy, operating systems, distributed systems, and the Web.

Privacy in data-driven systems

Her work studies how systems can offer more transparency and accountability when services, applications, and third-party trackers collect personal data. The aim is to design mechanisms that make privacy-preserving behavior more practical.

Research community

Geambasu joined Columbia after doctoral work at the University of Washington. Her work on cloud and mobile privacy connects theory, systems research, and deployed technology.
