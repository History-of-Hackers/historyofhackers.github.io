Juanita Moody

Moody’s record connects codebreaking and analysis to the operational reporting systems through which security information reaches decision-makers.

Juanita Moody joined the Army cryptologic organization in 1943 and remained in government cryptologic service for more than three decades. She rose through analytical and production leadership roles at NSA.

Cuban Missile Crisis

As a senior official responsible for signals intelligence on the region, Moody supervised daily production and reporting during the 1962 Cuban Missile Crisis. NSA’s account also credits her with innovations in reporting and direct support to the White House Situation Room.

In memoriam

Moody died in 2019. Her career demonstrates that analytical leadership and clear reporting are core parts of security practice.
