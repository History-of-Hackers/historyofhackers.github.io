Gerald Combs

Wireshark made detailed packet inspection broadly accessible. Its growth from Ethereal into a large open-source project shows how tools, contributors, documentation, and conferences form a durable technical community.

Gerald Combs created Ethereal, the open-source packet analyzer later renamed Wireshark. He began the tool in 1997 while working to understand network problems and learn more about networking.

From Ethereal to Wireshark

Ethereal attracted patches, dissectors, bug reports, and contributors. In 2006 the project adopted the Wireshark name, preserving the codebase community while moving beyond the Ethereal trademark.

A tool and a community

Wireshark is used for troubleshooting, protocol development, and security investigation. SharkFest, launched in 2008, is a place for the developer and user community to exchange packet-analysis practice.
