Thomas Pornin

Pornin’s work highlights an essential security lesson: cryptographic safety depends on implementations, protocol integration, and side-channel-aware engineering, not solely on primitive selection.

Thomas Pornin is a cryptographer and software engineer whose public work includes BearSSL, a TLS and cryptography implementation. BearSSL’s documentation explains its emphasis on small code, portability, and implementation choices relevant to side-channel resistance.

Implementation is a security boundary. Even a well-studied algorithm can fail in practice when key handling, timing behavior, protocol composition, or error processing is wrong.

Pornin has also published technical material on cryptographic engineering and practical implementations. His work is an example of the careful systems work required to carry cryptographic designs into real software.

He is included for contributions to implementation-focused cryptographic security.
