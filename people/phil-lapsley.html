Phil Lapsley

Lapsley preserves the social and technical history of phone phreaking, a foundational predecessor to later hacker culture and computer-security communities.

Phil Lapsley is an engineer and author. He co-authored RFC 977, which specified the Network News Transfer Protocol, and later wrote Exploding the Phone, a history of telephone phreaking.

Before the networked-computer era

Exploding the Phone documents people who explored and manipulated the telephone system in the 1950s through the 1970s. This history is central to understanding how technical curiosity, counterculture, and security concerns preceded modern computer hacking.
