John Markoff

Markoff helped bring the social history of hacking, network intrusion, and computer security to a broad readership through sustained technology reporting and books.

John Markoff is a technology journalist and author. His reporting for The New York Times covered the Internet, cybersecurity, science, and technology, and he co-wrote Cyberpunk: Outlaws and Hackers on the Computer Frontier with Katie Hafner.

Recording hacker culture

Cyberpunk documented prominent computer-intrusion cases and the surrounding culture in the late twentieth century. Markoff’s work is useful as contemporary journalism and as a book-length record of how hacking and computer security entered public debate.
