Alonzo Church

Church’s formal account of effective computation, developed alongside the work of his student Alan Turing, is a core foundation for understanding what computation can and cannot do—including the limits relevant to cryptography and security analysis.

Alonzo Church was a american mathematician and logician whose work on computability established enduring foundations for computer science and the formal analysis of algorithms.

Historical contribution

Church’s formal account of effective computation, developed alongside the work of his student Alan Turing, is a core foundation for understanding what computation can and cannot do—including the limits relevant to cryptography and security analysis.

In memoriam

Alonzo Church died in 1995. This archive remembers their contribution with care and credits the historical record on which this account relies.
