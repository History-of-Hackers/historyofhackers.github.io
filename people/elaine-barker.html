Elaine Barker

Barker’s standards work helped translate cryptographic research into durable guidance used by system designers and evaluators.

Elaine Barker is a cryptography specialist known for her work at the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology. Her work has contributed to guidance on cryptographic key management and the selection and use of cryptographic mechanisms.

Making cryptography operational

Cryptographic security depends not only on an algorithm but on key lifecycles, parameter choices, and implementation context. Barker’s NIST publications address these operational questions in guidance meant for practitioners and evaluators.
