Gary McGraw

McGraw made software security a concrete engineering discipline: design, testing, architecture, and governance are part of the product, not an afterthought.

Gary McGraw is a computer scientist, author, and software-security practitioner. He co-founded Cigital and later co-founded the Berryville Institute of Machine Learning; his work combines writing, research, and practical software-security programs.

Building security in

His books, including Building Secure Software, Exploiting Software, and Software Security, explain why secure software requires attention throughout development rather than a final testing pass.

Security practice at scale

His work on the Building Security In Maturity Model treats organizational security practices as something that can be observed and compared, moving application-security discussions toward repeatable engineering evidence.
