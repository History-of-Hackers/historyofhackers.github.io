Michael Backes

Backes has helped connect mathematically grounded security analysis with system and privacy research, while building major institutional capacity for cybersecurity research.

Michael Backes is a computer-security researcher associated with CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security. His research covers security, privacy, cryptography, and formal methods.

Security and privacy together

Backes’ work addresses the analysis of security protocols and privacy-preserving systems. This combination recognizes that a system can protect one property while failing another unless both are specified and evaluated.

Research institutions

He has also held leadership roles in German cybersecurity research, helping establish CISPA as a center for work spanning foundational and applied information security.
