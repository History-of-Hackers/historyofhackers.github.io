Tadayoshi Kohno

Kohno’s research makes security risks in consumer, medical, and civic technologies concrete, connecting technical analysis with human consequences.

Tadayoshi Kohno is a computer-security researcher and professor at the University of Washington. His work investigates security and privacy failures in technologies that affect people directly, including medical devices and connected consumer systems.

Human consequences

Kohno’s research is especially valuable because it treats security as a property of real devices in real settings. Public demonstrations and peer-reviewed work have helped engineers and policymakers understand why vulnerabilities in embedded systems can have safety implications.

Research community

Through scholarship and teaching, he has helped broaden security research beyond traditional desktop and network software.
