Gabriella Coleman

Coleman brings sustained ethnographic research to communities often represented only through policing, journalism, or technical accounts.

Gabriella (Biella) Coleman is an anthropologist and professor at Harvard University. Her research examines the politics, cultures, and ethics of hacking.

Books and research

Her books include Coding Freedom and Hacker, Hoaxer, Whistleblower, Spy, an ethnographic account of Anonymous. Her work connects hacker communities to questions of ethics, freedom, activism, and professionalization.
