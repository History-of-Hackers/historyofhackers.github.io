David L. Mills

Accurate, authenticated time is a security dependency for logging, certificates, distributed systems, and incident response. Mills made network time synchronization an enduring part of Internet infrastructure.

David L. Mills was a american computer engineer and internet pioneer who developed the Network Time Protocol (NTP) and taught computer security and networking at the University of Delaware.

Historical contribution

Accurate, authenticated time is a security dependency for logging, certificates, distributed systems, and incident response. Mills made network time synchronization an enduring part of Internet infrastructure.
